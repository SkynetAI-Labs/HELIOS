# GTM / Revenue Intelligence Agent

## H.E.L.I.O.S. Commercial Intelligence Module

The GTM Agent is a planned specialist AI agent within H.E.L.I.O.S., designed to support B2B sales, revenue intelligence, prospecting and go-to-market strategy.

## Core Capabilities

- Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) definition
- Target-account identification and prioritisation
- Company and account intelligence
- Buying-group and decision-maker mapping
- Buyer intent-signal detection
- Personalised prospecting research
- Daily GTM intelligence briefs
- Sales pipeline intelligence
- Competitor and market analysis
- Outreach experimentation
- Commercial opportunity scoring
- Decision memory and outcome analysis

## Proposed Architecture

1. N.O.V.A. gathers and analyses market intelligence.
2. The GTM Agent identifies and prioritises commercial opportunities.
3. H.E.L.I.O.S. coordinates agent workflows.
4. Human approval is required for consequential actions.
5. Connected tools execute approved workflows.
6. Outcomes are recorded to improve future decisions.

## Planned Integrations

- CRM platforms such as Salesforce
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator workflows
- Company and market intelligence APIs
- Email and outreach platforms
- Analytics and reporting dashboards
